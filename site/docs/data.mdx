---
title: Where your data lives
description: What stays on your Mac, what syncs, and what reaches whom.
---

| what                                   | where                                             | syncs? |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Connections and their tokens           | `connections.json` in Flux's settings folder      | never  |
| Projects, saved queries, history       | the settings folder, or a workspace's `.flux`     | never  |
| Streams and endpoints already seen     | the cache folder                                  | never  |
| Window layout, open tabs               | `workspace.json`, this Mac only                   | never  |
| Appearance, keymap, console and grid settings | your account                               | yes    |

Flux talks to your sources from your Mac; nothing you read is sent anywhere. It checks our update server for new versions at launch and hourly. The [Privacy Policy](https://flux.emreb.dev/privacy) has the full picture.

**Help › Show Settings Folder in Finder** opens the folder; deleting it removes everything Flux kept on this Mac.
