---
title: Monitor
description: A live dashboard of a connection's logs.
---

**Monitor**, under Connection Explorer in the left stripe, is a live view of a connection: throughput, failures, error rate, p95, hosts, levels and the failures that repeat, for one stream or all of them, updating as the logs arrive.

Monitor is in beta. What it shows depends on what the stream logs: durations give p95, statuses and levels give failures.
